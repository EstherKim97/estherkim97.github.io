## SeaTRAC TB Hack Day — Feb 11, 2026 (UCSF participation)

Collaborative hack day focused on profiling clinical trial TB datasets to analyze drug treatment responses. The event provided datasets, R resources, and remote RStudio servers for participants.

- Event repo: https://github.com/agartland/seatrac-hackday-2026
- Goal: collaborate on TB clinical trial dataset profiling and share reproducible analyses.
- Format: short dataset intro, unstructured hacking time, and end-of-day readouts.
- Locations: Seattle, UCSF Mission Bay (MH-2105), Texas Biomed, and virtual participation.
- Communications: Slack channel (organizers provided invite) and Zoom for virtual coordination.

Notes: I participated in the UCSF TB Hack Day; ran exploratory code and small analyses using the event repository and provided R resources.
