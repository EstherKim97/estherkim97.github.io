## AI-powered Biomedical Knowledge Graph

KnowledgeGraph is an evidence-intelligence pipeline that turns PubMed literature into structured claims, knowledge graphs, and concise research briefs. It includes entity normalization, relation extraction, and graph construction to enable queryable biomedical insights.

**Status:** Open source (GitHub) — technical repo available.

**Highlights:** structured evidence, claim extraction, research-brief generation.
