## What I did

I developed Target Product Profiles (TPPs) for MAC Copilot, a digital decision-support tool for dementia care developed by UCSF's Memory and Aging Center. A TPP sets out what the product must do, for whom, and what evidence it needs, so the team can plan development backwards from those requirements.

## How

I worked with clinicians and researchers to assess market and regulatory feasibility, drawing on my four years in drug regulatory affairs to judge which evidence a regulator would expect.
