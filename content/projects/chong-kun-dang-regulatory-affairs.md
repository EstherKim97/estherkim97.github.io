## Programs

I worked on biosimilars of ranibizumab and risankizumab and on a novel EGFR/c-MET bispecific antibody, with submissions to MFDS (Korea) and FDA.

## Product approval and post-approval change: ranibizumab biosimilar

I built a terminology map across departments and ran pre-submission check meetings for the marketing application, which MFDS approved in October 2022. After approval, I worked on the post-approval change from vial to prefilled syringe.

## IND: novel EGFR/c-MET bispecific antibody

When some data were not yet final, I set priorities for which data to lock first so the program could enter Phase 1.

## FDA pre-IND: risankizumab biosimilar

One of two people on the submission. I compared CROs as part of the preparation.

## Across all programs

I shared regular summaries of new regulatory guidelines with the team. Every submission I owned went in on time without being returned.
